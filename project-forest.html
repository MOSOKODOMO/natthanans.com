THE PROPOSITION

A place to pause, learn and restore.

A seasonal workshop campsite for trekkers explores the relationship between an old mining landscape, ecological restoration and small-scale construction.

THE QUESTION

Support an educational trekking route within a landscape shaped by historic mining.

DESIGN THINKING

A clear idea.
Spatial consequences.

Use the campsite as a place to learn about restoration, with a design informed by field visits, local materials and construction studies.
